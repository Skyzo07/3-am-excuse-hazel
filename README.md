One line under 200 characters: the most creative excuse a hardware wallet gives for refusing to sign a transaction at 3 AM.

> No. The last time you woke me at 3 AM, you blamed me by breakfast — I'm not signing your regret before you've even had it.

`main.ts` writes that line exactly to `/output/entry.txt`.
